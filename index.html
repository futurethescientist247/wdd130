Nigeria

Nigeria is a country in West Africa and the most populous country on the continent, with more than 200 million people. Its capital is Abuja, and its largest city is Lagos. It is bordered by Benin, Niger, Chad, and Cameroon, and has a coastline on the Atlantic Ocean. Nigeria is known for its many cultures and languages, its music and film industries, and its oil resources.
